# MiYooSDR 0.7.0

Two new ways to look at the 2.4 GHz band: a scope that reads one frequency about
3,000 times a second, and a persistence display.

## New

- **Scope mode.** R2 at the 5 MHz zoom, or `--scope MHZ`, reads the frequency under
  the cursor over and over instead of sweeping: about 3,100 readings a second on
  the Mini Plus, where a sweep comes back to a frequency only every 40 to 200 ms.
  The screen shows a rolling trace, the last burst stretched out with a millisecond
  axis, and burst timing: how many, how long ago, how far apart, and the hits in
  the last one. A hit is a reading well above a background that adapts to the
  noise; hits less than 50 ms apart make one burst.
- Scope controls: Left / Right move one 312.5 kHz bin, SELECT + Left / Right
  change the trace length from 0.05 to 10 s, A holds the screen, B clears the
  count, L2 goes back to the zoom.
- **Scope recordings.** X in the scope records every reading to
  `rec/scope-<date>-<time>.csv`. The browser marks them `[SCOP]` and plays them on
  the scope's own screen, with seeking and 0.25x to 8x speed. `--replay` plays
  them too.
- **Persistence display.** SELECT + B now cycles the lower panel through the
  waterfall, channel occupancy and persistence: how often each level has shown up
  at each frequency, so steady carriers become lines, busy channels clouds and
  rare hits dots. `--panel N` starts on a panel, and the settings file remembers
  it.

## Verified

On the same Mini Plus and stock driver as 0.6.0:

- The scope read 3,067 to 3,106 times a second at 2426.06 MHz, Bluetooth LE
  advertising channel 38, and caught bursts there as short as a single 0.3 ms
  reading, most likely advertising (the scope measures energy; it does not decode).
  A scope recording replayed on the Mini and on a PC gave byte-identical screens.
- R2 from the 5 MHz zoom opened the scope at the cursor, SELECT + Right lengthened
  the trace, and L2 went back to the zoom. Live sweeps resumed with no I/O errors,
  and Wi-Fi came back after every run.
- The persistence panel built up at the 5 MHz zoom, showing a steady emitter near
  2428 MHz as a bright streak, and SELECT + B cycled it to the waterfall.

Automated tests grew from 59 to 72. The scope's reader thread also ran under
ThreadSanitizer, and the new screens under AddressSanitizer and
UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer.

## Install

Extract `miyoosdr-0.7.0.zip` to the root of the SD card. It installs
`App/MiYooSDR` and `App/MiYooSDR Replay`. Existing recordings and settings are
not included in the archive and are kept.

Packet capture remains a separate command-line utility in `research/`; see the
[driver research notes](../driver-research.md). No kernel or driver replacement
is included.
